---
title: im better off being a memory than a reality
---

pretty much the title, i imagine myself as a memory and all i can think of is positive.
i see a relatively carefree guy with a bunch of friends that tries to hold himself up to an exhaustingly high moral standard, a curious man that loves to learn things, just trying to make it through life. 
i hope that's how ill be remembered, WITH EMPHASIS ON MY LOVE FOR CATS. i hope people think of how much i love cats when they look back at my life.
scarcity adds value, the only reason life is valuable is because you only get it once and you get about 120ish years absolute maximum. there is nothing at this time that you can do in order to live further than that no matter how hard you try. maybe science will change that, maybe it will not, but at this point in time that's the way it is and that's the way it's always been hence the love for life. 
the same way people do not see the whole weight of the existence of a person until they're well and truly gone, meaning dead. because that's the point of no return. you can atleast hang onto stupid hope that a person that left you may come back but you cannot bring a dead man back to life and hence they become suddenly so much more important. people often miraculously "love" and "miss" you after you're dead while killing you when you're alive. it's only and ONLY the fact that you're dead that makes you so vacuously valuable.
i dont know if the care was ever real, i dont know how people will react, i dont know if people thought this would spiral this fast and this hard into me actually taking my own life. 
well i dont fucking care. bye.
