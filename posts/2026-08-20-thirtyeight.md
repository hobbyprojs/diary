title: don't 
---

i just don't want to
i dont want to anything. i dont want to wake up i dont want to eat i dont want to sleep
i just want to do nothing at all i dont even want to move i want to lay down and become a statue
my brain is working noticeably slower than usual and it's actually making mistakes
my thoughts are incoherent and foggy
i wanted to be great but deep down i had this inherent will against it. im afraid of being good over a certain extent 
im afraid of beginning to chase perfection because maybe i can reach it
but i dont want "perfect" i want real and perfect can never be real. i don't want to be smart i would never wish that upon anyone
i don't want to be knowledgeable I don't want the burden of wisdom 
yet something inside me craves it and I can't stop it. i can't stop learning. i dont want to. i don't want any more knowledge 
im of the belief that you only brag about your intelligence if you're insecure about it, a genuinely smart man never feels the need to prove himself 
or one up you because he knows what he is. but as humans do they tend to give more weight to the loud not-so-smart man over the silent smart one
what even is being smart? it sure isn't knowing things because that would just be knowledge. you can have all the knowledge in the world and still be stupid
knowing how to use that knowledge, maybe, is being smart. or so i think. i dont know. i dont know anything. i dont know even the most fundamental of things. im stupid
as i should've always been. i do not know anything, i speak no language, i have no clue what 1+1 is, my brain simply does not work.
grades mean nothing. the grades are a façade. i don't know anything. not even the most basic of things.
i love my city. i love my state. i love my country. i love other countries as well. i love the world and i wish peace upon it
i wish to see everyone grow in leaps and bounds and perhaps leave the world a better place. not me though. im scared of making the world a better place. i dont know why.
i don't want to contribute. i can, i don't want to. i really really don't want to. neither the good nor the bad i want nothing to do with it.
i hope the hungry and helpless find their comfort. i hope the ukrainians and russians end the war and reconcile. i hope the starving orphaned children in gaza see much better days
i hope the armenians and the azerbaijanis will be on good terms again. i hope the innocent that suffer through all of these events, the ones that had nothing to do with it yet
bear the consequences, get relief. 
blessed are those who mourn for they will find comfort
it makes no sense to me that i see death as a sort of new beginning, the "actual" beginning while simultaneously believing it's just lights out after. just ceasing to exist
the scientist in me says cessation of brain activity means there won't be a me anymore so it isn't even a void it's just not being. like there's no concept of me to ask where i am
i use metaphor because admittedly it's a bit comforting and i dont really see a reason not to although i dont believe it
well. the more honest answer would be saying we don't know what happens after. maybe as the noose tightens and less and less blood flows through my carotids ill be exactly where i want to be
- maybe ill be in her arms after all of this. maybe it'll all just be a bad dream. maybe none of the bad part ever really happened and i wake up in early march and everything's fine again and i actually have days to look forward to
or maybe i wake up at my first birthday party in my mom's arms. maybe the voice of god will tell me i can finally rest now
maybe ill be back to the carefree child i used to be, unaware of the world and its sorrows, never having been cursed with knowledge or the ability to genuinely love.
or be anointed for a mission way beyond what is possible for me.
i think my brain has officially accepted what's about to come because yesterday i dreamt of (yes i slept for 2 whole hours, turns out i dont have fatal familial insomAnia) some white elephant? the fuck??
isn't white elephant a gift somebody would give that's so high maintenance that you'd be better off not getting it
something about people in thailand gifting a white elephant to people they dont like because they're sacred so you can't get rid of them and have to take care of them but you'll bankrupt yourself in the process something like that i dont really know like i said i know absolutely nothing 
but i have to say that is sort of poetic and i did not expect my brain to come up with such a beautiful analogy all of a sudden. i wasn't even thinking that way and i did nothing to do with elephants in the past few months but out of the blue it pops this thing out - maybe that white elephant represents my life, a sacred, well meaning [citation needed] gift that ends up just dismantling me
what makes it even more beautiful is in that dream the elephant was huge perhaps analogous to a long lifespan and they kept it locked away in this place but every once in a frequent while like daily it escapes and wreaks havoc on the surroundings yet they don't harm it they just hide during it's rampage and pray it'll get bored and go back into its little enclosure because it's rare and too precious to hurt/kill. since when did my brain start producing actual art? this shit can be applied to anything. that elephant could be anything. my life. my love. anything.
well while that was subtle and poetic, my dream today was a bit crude and direct, in a part of the dream i had i was going somewhere and saw a rope shop. wtf. rope shop. i dont think those exist but my mind made them up cuz why not and obviously me being me i dropped my work and went into that rope store mind you my dreams are less like movies and more like games, i have some control over the choices i make and what i do so like that stopping and entering the store was an active decision i made in the dream, i think this is called lucid dreaming or something idk just to reiterate i do not know anything whatsoever
sometimes the dreams feel indistinguishable from reality sometimes im actually aware that im in a dream not like "hey you're dreaming right now" per se but more like "this isn't the world i was in" which basically can only mean one other thing - that im dreaming. there are few quirks and stuff, recurring places in dreams, recurring people... i still remember the smell of that street i see in my dreams often. it smells like comfort and nostalgia, there's shops set up all along and there's this big grocery store sort of thing with about 3 floors if im not mistaken and over on the other street is the old man with the glasses i forgot what shop he runs like what kind of shop but it smells good
i also have continuation dreams that pick up exactly where they ended, sometimes in consecutive days and sometimes its one on day 1 then i forget about it then a month later boom that again ive had one of those that comes up like every half a year or something i forgot what it was even about i only remember the driveway and some kids playing there near that big building
sorry i digressed hard i went into the rope store and looked around for a nice one there was a semi thick purple one and a thinner yellow one and i was actually thinking in the dream isnt thinner better because it blocks bloodflow better or something but yeah i ended up enquiring about the price and this part is fuzzy but i think i thought it was too expensive and walked out telling myself ill find some for cheaper later
so yeahhh that was that, a garbled mess of my thoughts
