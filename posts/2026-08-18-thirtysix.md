im so obsessed with the "after" part that im obsessing over every little detail... it is a once in a lifetime 
thing but god damn who even plans this much for the LAST thing they do in their lives.
had i not worried about the "after" which I wont even be here to see i would've hung myself right here 50 times over!
had i not been worried about my body being found i would've jumped right off a tall building long ago and got this over and done with
once and for all
but no im obsessed with getting this right im obsessed with correctness im obsessed with perfection im obsessed with making something out of this.

but i need to atleast find that spot. i need to atleast die with dignity. i need to do SOMETHING i dont know what before i die.
