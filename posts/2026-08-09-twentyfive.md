---
title: accursed
---

indeed we are both blessed and cursed, cursed to live a life in a world like this and blessed that it isn't permanent.
the greatest tragedy in life is not death but latching and holding onto life.
money runs the world, the world has no god except for money. we are all mindless machines that live, work, and die, and know nothing good except for money.
most people do not want to see a better future for the world, they wish to see a better future for their own selves where they have loads of money.
there is nothing of more value than money in the world. if you have money people will look at you as a higher being, they'll treat you well, your work gets done.
otherwise you're a subhuman. your value, your worth, it all comes not from your humanity but from how much your net worth is.
the rich have absurd amounts of money and at a point it becomes a thing to show off without any actual difference in daily life - a billionaire and a multi billionaire
realistically have no difference in their daily lives, they both live in the same luxury the only difference is how much they have in the bank.
what happens to that money? it gets invested, reinvested, then re-reinvested over and over again to keep growing just to go and sit back in that bank account again, serving no purpose.
this is not saving. it's piling up money to serve as a symbol of status, to rank worldwide, to have your name be heard.
at the end of the day you do not take anything to the other world with you except for your deeds and doings.
why be so afraid of death? people are cryogenically freezing their bodies in hopes that future science can bring them back to life. why this attachment to the world knowing it isn't here to stay?
no righteous man is afraid of death or the judgement that comes after.
