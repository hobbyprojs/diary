title: i feel happy
---

it's over. it's finally over. i feel happy, i know this only lasts a few minutes but i feel happy.
my anhedonia decided to give me a break and i feel okay now.
i dont know if it's because i finally found what ligature I'll use or because ive taken actions that won't allow me to go back to live even if i changed my mind last second (don't reeeeally know how to feel about that) but it's over. and you know what? it isn't even that bad. it's not bad at all. i feel so light. i feel bliss i feel like im on top of the world i feel a sense of calm that i pray will continue for the remaining 27 or so days i have left knowing damn well it wont.
it's okay. it really is. it isn't so bad. 27 days. i can do it. 
then ill be free. ill be happy again. ill be anything but sad, ill either be happy or dead. it hurts but it hurts so good, im no masochist but you know what this is beautiful and im crying tears of joy not of grief. i lived a fullish life, i played my part, i did what was expected of me up until now, i got to see the sun rise and set, i got to meet people, i got to do things, and now it's time to pack it up. everybody everywhere ever is on a timer and mine just happened to run out earlier than the rest. what's so bad about that? maybe this is all im destined to, maybe this is all that i have written under my name. that's fine by me. there are certain things in life you just can't change and trying to go against nature to change them only leads to increased suffering. acceptance is what sets you free. i will just sit there and let it happen 

my decline is going incredibly well i went into a semi catatonic state where my mind just fixates on something and i can't and dont want to do anything. i literally physically dont want to move. the inertia is unbelievable i do not want to get up from the place where im sat and i do not want to open my mouth. i dont want to talk. i just want to turn invisible, i just want to be there but not fully there, i just want to lay down and die. im hearing a lot of things but not listening to any of them. im requiring people to tell me something multiple times not because I can't hear them but because it buys me time to enqueue processing whatever words they said to me. i feel like answering i dont know to even the things i know just so id be left the fuck alone. just leave me to die. i dont want to eat, i dont want to drink, i dont want to sleep. i dont want to wake up, i dont want to work, i dont want to think. i dont even want to kill myself i wish somebody else would do it for me.
im not wincing anymore. im not smiling either. im in this state of okayness. it feels like a calm morning that has neither gone wrong or right because it's just the start of the day still.
my emotional regulation center is doing its work of trying to comfort me by replaying emotions that arrest my anxiety. i think im just not on edge rather than happy actually i dont know how either of those are supposed to feel but it may be one of those. i dont know. i dont know and i dont care. ill be dead soon anyway
i no longer see tomorrow as work. i dont see it as an obstruction. i see it as a pathway i have to walk first in order to get to my destination of death. everything's according to plan as it should be
i wonder if, if there is a god would he punish me more for premeditating my murder of self this hard? i dont even hold a grudge against myself. i dont hate myself i hate what life does to me. every culture sees this as an act of self murder not self killing. khud-kushi, aatma-hatya.
