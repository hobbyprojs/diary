Dear God. i thank you for granting me life. i thank you for allowing me to see. i thank you for showing the beauty of your greatest creation, the universe, and the putridity (is that a word?) of your definitely-not-the-greatest
creation, humans. i love you Lord with my whole heart i just wish you had shown the tiniest bit more mercy on me. yet i am still grateful. this is just part of what is already written and im only playing my part in it according to your rules.
people have given you titles from he who he is, ar rahman, ar raheem, al malik, the brahman, the eeshwar, waheguru, ik onkar, ahura mazda, arihant and many more. they left out one thing. you
are the seer. you are the doer. you are the mover. i pray you show more mercy on my companions-in-suffering. i wish you make this world a better place. a more tolerable, less isolating place.
i see the beauty of the trees, the skies, the planets, the heavens above. your handiwork is above all else, you are without a doubt the best of creators. it is nothing but astonishing to me to see how far your children have come and continue to go.




"دنیا کی محفلوں سے اکتا گیا ہوں یا ربکیا لطف انجمن کا جب دل ہی بجھ گیا ہو"
