this is extremely difficult.
i cannot keep living only during the very few "highs".
there is something systematically and fundamentally wrong with the way this "life" thing works for me.
was i even alive post-2019? did the apocalypse actually happen and we're actually living in hell now? 
i used to present this question jokingly given how bad life has gotten after 2019 but now I can't help but wonder if it may be actually true 
this is not bad. this is a fundamental alteration of what is considered "life" and "the world" for the worse.
and i cannot believe how few people are outspoken about this seethingly apparent difference.
just think about it. juxtapose life in 2018 against now. do you see the difference?
how far fetched is it to believe this isn't even reality? time does not feel real anymore. to be honest nothing feels real anymore.
i know most people that greet me with a smile probably bitch about me behind my back, i know the people that pretend to dislike me 
actually hate me instead, i know this entire thing is a stupid, stupid show. this "life" thing is a play. the world is a circus and i am the jester for still participating in it.
of your existence, your life is the shortest period. why care? no, good does not win, it never does. atleast not in this world.
i hate this stupid guy so fucking much. it's me. i hate him so much. infact if i and he were different people i would bash his fucking skull in. i hate him so much.
i hate everything he does. i hate him waking up, i hate him walking, i hate him talking, i hate him existing.
this calamity will only end with him ceasing to exist.
i do not want things to get better. i want things worse. much worse than they are now.
i want afflictions. i want to be hurt more, i want more people to leave me, i want to feel even worse. i want to be dragged to the deepest pits of hell and left there to die.
the only thing i want is the ability to take myself out of this joke of an existence. perhaps then ill feel less guilty about "wasting my life" or ruining the apparently good life of a smart man.
i will throw it away. i will. so what? i need to stay alive because im "smart"? you're underestimating my stupidity. 
i refuse to be chained to this world because i could potentially add to it. when i was younger i had all the will but no ability to do that, now i have all the ability and absolutely no will.
the human race may further itself, it may not, or it may in the best case implode on itself and rid the world of a mistake.
