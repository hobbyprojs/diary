---
title: isn't death odd?
---

odd as in strange. it's such a seemingly simple process, your bodily processes simply.... stop. your brain doesnt receive oxygen rich blood for a while? gone.
your heart stops pumping for a bit? gone. everything we do everyday is actively trying to prevent death. it sounds strange to me when said out loud.
everything is trying to kill you 24/7 and you casually prevent that from happening, the natural direction of EVERYTHING in the world is towards disorder because of entropy,
isn't that crazy? you have to provide energy to keep things in order. constantly. 
then comes the dreaded death... the great equalizer. the only thing we're guaranteed in life is death. you were not guaranteed to be born - infact the fact that you're what and who you are,
with your very attributes, with everything about you the way it is now is incomprehensibly improbable. the slightest variation in genes would make you an entirely different person.
so you were not guaranteed... you were a thing of chance. a coin flip, except the coin has like what a trillion sides, not gonna exhaust myself doing the math now no thank you, may be more than the trillions
but death truly is guaranteed. every person ever born and ever will be born will die. rich, poor, good, bad, beautiful, ugly.... all of us taste death.
some die in silly ways, like chrysippus dying of laughter at his own joke. some go in a fireball of glory, like martyrdom or sacrifice. but they're both... dead.
death seems to not discriminate whatsoever. you may argue that the rich live longer because of better healthcare hence death does discriminate but id say that isnt death discriminating,
because if you didnt intervene and you just let nature take its course the rich man would be dead as well.
from the moment there was a you till now your heart never stopped beating. (unless you've ever gone into cardiac arrest/asystole and have been defibrillated/resuscitated)
your brain did not stop functioning. yet the moment theres a cessation of blood flow to your brain it simply goes bye bye. you go unconscious in seconds. irreversible tissue death in minutes.
autolysis. your dead tissue literally starts eating itself. then you just become one with what you came from, the ground. 
that's how quick it can end. it brings to my mind the teachings found in the bhagavad gita, dhammapada, jainism, even the abrahamic religions that teach that attachment, or want, is the root of grief
this secured future you're living in right now may cease the next second. we're truly holding onto nothing. one snap and it's all gone. 
it feels odd to die. this is my home. this is the only home i've ever known, and i am now looking for another home, if there is one...
it feels like im leaving it behind because i am. that's such a strange feeling. im deeply in love with the world, i love this world. i love this planet. i love the sunrise and the sunset.
i love the clouds, the birds, the animals, the trees everything that makes up this beautiful planet. i won't be here tomorrow but the sun will rise. the birds will chirp. the moon will shine.
the rivers will flow. the humans will go about their days as usual. you truly have nobody in this universe except for yourself.
this heart of mine that kept itself coordinated for the entire time of my life will stop beating. this brain of mine that won't ever shut up to the point where i can hear the echo of my own thoughts will go silent.
that's so weird to me. to even consider my ever so loud brain going silent is so weird. my neurons wont fire anymore, what makes me me won't exist anymore. replaced by a calm cold corpse with no soul.
am i overreacting? what am i even doing? is this an apt response expected of a man that's seen the front and back of hell in a couple months?
i don't think i deal with trauma. i just push it away. i never want to face it.
my brain immediately numbs or dumbs me to avoid pain. if i go through something really bad i just plain stop feeling. my brain shuts itself down. i can still have some cognitive function although its extremely slowed.
i don't talk, i don't think, i don't... function.
or the dumbing part, i push it to the absolute side. refusing to even acknowledge it. okay that happened? okay. anyways. but i never let go of it. it's always at the back of my head, constantly running... but i avoid it.
i never confront it because i can't. it would be destructive if i did.
like when dad was in the ICU... he could've literally died there. like i genuinely could've lost my father at that moment. although well i did lose my father. it isn't like he remembers who any of us is anyway.
all that remains is some stupid hope that i hang on to that somehow beyond the large areas of hypodensity indicating damaged tissue and visible cerebral atrophy on the CT and the bleed in the thalamus there would somehow be a miraculous
return of motor ability and memory sometime into the future but as time goes on im coming to terms with reality. but that very possibility just shakes me. i seriously and actually could have lost my father right there.
any one of the times we spoke to the neurosurgeon or the critical care doctor could've been the time we received the bad news. i would have certainly not been here now if that did happen, which is idiotic of me because my family loses a person
and i just add to that grief and take myself out too instead of supporting them? am i actually stupid? but am i supposed to subject myself to this eternal torture for my family? they can and will survive without me... id just make it a bit easier a few years down the line
like a long term investment. is it a worthwhile tradeoff is what we must think of. an appreciable bit of hardship for my family for my forever relief? ugh. tempting. of course i would prefer not to put anyone through this but i feel like things will be fine after
im just overthinking this. nobody will even think about me twice. people will forget i existed.
yuck. honestly i dont even want to think about that. makes me nauseous. the fallout is not my problem to deal with, whatever happens after is whatever happens after i wont be here to deal with it im just gone doing dead people stuff i guess.
one more problem i noticed is that there's two sides of me, one side punching the air and begging me to hold on a bit longer to experience more of life while the other side is so sick and tired of all this anticipating and wants me to just get this over with.
it wants me to carry out the plan asap or abandon it as a whole. knowing i made my mind up and the second option is out of question, it's forcing me to carry it out quick. it gets extremely irritated every passing day that i keep living rather than just ending it once and forall.
i feel almost, how do i put this, like compelled to keep my promise like i feel held accountable for going through with this ughhh i cant find the word it feels like not going with it would be a sin its engraved into me to do it 
should i stop going to college for a while? to spend time with myself? but that would be so suspicious... but my friends, they make it better, why would i avoid them? god. its such a mess. why cant i be one in the millions dying everyday of one thing or another. freak car accident.
crossing the road. shock. sudden cardiac death. whatever. gosh. i just don't want this to be at my own hands. i don't want my own blood on my hands.
i feel so uncomfortable. i feel like going somewhere i dont even know where. i feel like doing something i dont even know what. all i know is i dont like this one bit.
