---
title: grief
---

brain stroke is one of the absolute worst things a person and the people around them can go through.
you know, whenever i think of "worst diseases/conditions in existence" the absolute first two things to pop up into my head are prion diseases and rabies.
i never thought to add stroke to that list because my mind was only ever fixed on the mortality aspect of things and it never occurred to me that there are fates worse than death itself.
locked in syndrome, stroke, many more.... i take great respite in the fact that he survived the acute event but the fallout from it is a struggle too.
seeing a person you love be normal just the very morning of the event, without any symptoms at all, first be on the brink of death, then turn into a person i dont recognize...
it's a heavier weight than i'd like to admit. i feel my heart crack when he moves his left arm and leg in no discernible pattern because that's the only form of communication he has.
i can never understand that pain. i do not know what it's like to have a lot to say but not be physically able to say it and just flail your arms around hoping somebody would get the point.
seeing him in such a helpless situation makes me feel terrible. what really plucked the strings of my heart was about a month or two ago when he called me over to say something.
i sat down next to him and listened. he was struggling to speak, i could see his lips move but no sound came out. i encouraged him to take a deep breath and then attempt to speak again.
after all that struggle the words that came out of his mouth - "i will die", in the sense of "let me die". i felt sad, really sad, but i understood. i understand. everytime i hear mom yell at him
i feel this disturbance - on one hand i understand mom's frustration, on the other i understand dad's inability. maybe he genuinely can't do what's being asked of him.
maybe he truly doesn't recognize any of us - strangers surrounding him telling him what to do. that would be terrifying for anyone, god bless his soul.
maybe there are pains, worries, concerns - things he's unable to communicate. i see him trying to kick, punch, bite and all sorts of other stuff with us and think maybe he's actually lost his sanity.
but it may just be his inability to communicate. the distress must be made known somehow, and when you can't speak and people dont understand you you take extreme measures
i know this isn't him. i know he isn't doing this to hurt us or agitate us. this is just the unfortunate consequence of an inevitable event, but it does make me sit down and think
after what magnitude of suffering does life decide you've had enough? is there any? yes, life was terrible before, but atleast i didn't have to see my father suffer everyday like this.
atleast i didn't have to see mom struggle everyday like this. funnily enough one of the things we told ourselves to get by was that we may not have had money but atleast everyone in the family was healthy
as opposed to people that are sick. now that is gone as well. dad, i love you. im proud of you. your life is a testament of your hardwork, you are a strong man. surviving a stroke of that magnitude is not something that just happens.
may you, mom, and sis have an equal share of the rest of my lifespan that i'm giving up. may you recover well, may they both never have to deal with ailments, may all of you live long happy fulfilling lives.
well it is what it is. maybe we misunderstood death? we have this inherent belief that death is bad and the worst thing but maybe in reality death is the most merciful thing trying to free you from the horrors of life?
death gets a bad rep because it separates people but maybe it isn't all that bad. maybe death is on our side and life on the opposite. i wouldn't say that's too far fetched to consider.
a merciful god cannot possibly want to see his creatures suffer - he cannot want to force us to live through this much pain. perhaps that is why he left this door open and let this option exist, maybe this is his mercy.
well, let me end this post with something important - keep your fucking blood pressure under control. hypertension is called the silent killer for a reason. hypertension, diabetes - never ignore these. get checked up FREQUENTLY.
the root cause of hypertension for most hypertensive people is obstructive sleep apnea, characterized sometimes by snoring, i say sometimes because it can present without snoring. get a sleep study done. get a CPAP/BIPAP machine.
don't let it get this out of hand. i may now sound hypocritical saying this but don't smoke or drink. really bad for you. take care of yourself and your loved ones before it's too late, you can NEVER turn time back. you have a limited amount of time with them.
